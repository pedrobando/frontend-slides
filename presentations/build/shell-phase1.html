Day 2 · The business

It's a business.
It runs on four slices.

Sales

Turn people into signed clients.

Covers: Buyers first call · Zero to 45 · Showings to offer · Expireds and the listing call · At the table.

Marketing

Get seen, bring the leads.

Covers: Personal brand and storefront · Your social week · Hooks and video · El motor · Capture the DM.

Admin

Run the system so nothing drops.

Covers: Dashbrew stages · Seguimiento rhythm · Your day on the calendar · Signed to live in 48h · Offer to keys.

Accountability

Know your number, score it weekly.

Covers: Look back at 2026 · Your 2027 number · Monday planning report · Ads scoreboard · Sealed card.

You set the center once. You run the slices every week. Sales and Marketing grow it. Admin and Accountability keep it.
