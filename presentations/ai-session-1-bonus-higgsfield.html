Why a project and not one-off chats? A folder with its own CLAUDE.md means Claude already knows your brand, your voice, the 9:16 format and the rules every time. You stop re-explaining. You also keep every idea, script and winning prompt in one place you can reuse next week.Un proyecto organizado: Claude ya sabe quién eres antes de que le pidas nada.
Claude writes the recipe. Higgsfield cooks it. You serve it.Advanced, later: Higgsfield also offers a connector so Claude can call it directly. Start with copy and paste. Learn the steps first.

Before You Post

Check It. Then Share It.

Fair Housing cleanNo mention of protected classes. Describe the home and your service, not who should live there.
Photos I ownOnly your photos or ones you have rights to. No random web images.
Brokerage name visiblereThought Real Estate shows on the story where advertising rules require it.
Numbers are realEvery stat, price and date comes from MLS or another source I checked. No invented data.
AI is disclosed when neededIf the clip could be mistaken for real footage, label it as AI-generated or add a note.
Hook in the first secondWatch it on mute with the text on. Is it clear? Is it under 15 seconds?
Saved and filedVideo in 05-Final. Winning prompt in prompts-that-worked.md.
I reviewed itI watched the whole clip. Claude and Higgsfield never post for me.

Never

  • Never make a fake person and present them as a real client, agent or testimonial.
  • Never generate a house and present it as a real listing or your sold home.
  • Never use someone's face, voice or photo without permission.
  • Never post stats, prices or market claims you cannot back up.
  • Never skip your review. You are responsible for what goes out.
Rule of thumb: AI makes the motion. You supply the truth.La IA pone el movimiento. Tú pones la verdad.
