Diagnosis

When a Department Closes, You Feel It.

You can’t really close a department. You can only stop showing up for it. Here’s what that costs.

Closed
Business Development
Feast or famine.
Your pipeline runs on luck, and past clients call another agent when it’s time to sell.
Closed
Marketing
Nobody new knows you sell homes.
Your business only grows as fast as your contact list.
Closed
Sales
Leads go cold.
You pay for conversations and never get the signature.
Closed
Admin
Deals and money slip away.
Missing files at closing, a pipeline you can’t see, and a tax bill you didn’t plan for.
Which one did you feel this year?
Hold on to the answer. You’ll use it in the lab.
