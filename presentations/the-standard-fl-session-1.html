The File Outlives the Deal

“

“Each broker shall preserve at least one legible copy of all books, accounts, and records pertaining to her or his real estate brokerage business for at least 5 years from the date of receipt of any money, fund, deposit, check, or draft entrusted to the broker or, in the event no funds are entrusted to the broker, for at least 5 years from the date of execution by any party of any listing agreement, offer to purchase, … or any other written or verbal agreement which engages the services of the broker.”

— Fla. Stat. §475.5015
En cristiano: If a client ever signed anything, or money ever touched the broker’s hands, the file stays five years — whether or not the deal ever closed.

Act 2 · §475.278

Three Relationships. One Presumption.

Florida gives you exactly three ways to work with a buyer or seller. If nothing is in writing — you’re the first one.

The Default — Presumed
Transaction Broker
Limited representation of buyer, seller, or both — no fiduciary duty to either.
§475.278(1)(b): “It shall be presumed that all licensees are operating as transaction brokers” unless another relationship is established in writing.
In Writing Only
Single Agent
Full fiduciary representation of one party only.
Requires the statutory Single Agent Notice, in writing, before or at listing / representation — or before showing.
Rare
No Brokerage Relationship
You represent no one — but still owe honesty, material-fact disclosure & accounting.
Established in writing. Three baseline duties never go away.
If you can’t name your relationship on a deal, Florida already named it for you.

Act 2 · §475.278(3)(b)–(c)

When the Law Demands Paper

Two legal requirements. Zero drafting — the language lives in our forms.

Single Agent
Duties disclosed in writing before listing, representation, or first showing — whichever occurs first.
§475.278(3)(b)
Single Agent → Transaction Broker Mid-Deal
The principal’s written consent, signed or initialed, before the transition.
§475.278(3)(c)
You never draft this language — it’s already printed inside our documents:
📄Listing Agreement — Single Agent edition (notice built in)
📄Buyer Broker Agreement — Single Agent (notice built in)
📄Consent to Transition to Transaction Broker (standalone form)
When would I ever transition?
Your single-agent buyer falls in love with your own single-agent listing. You can’t owe undivided loyalty to both sides of one deal — so BOTH sign the consent, and you become a transaction broker for that transaction.
A phone call is not consent. A text that says “ok” is not consent. Signed or initialed. On file.

Buyer Broker Agreement: Before You Tour, You Sign

Florida Law
—Ch. 475 does not require a written buyer agreement

✓Written agreement before touring — including live virtual tours

The Agreement ≠ The Relationship
01Sign nothing about agency? Florida still presumes you’re a transaction broker — automatically.

No signed agreement = No tour It’s also how you get paid
This isn’t friction. It’s the moment your buyer learns what you do and what you earn. Clarity is the brand.
