What Happens in Two Seconds

Three Silent Questions. In This Order.

Nobody says them out loud. Every brain asks them anyway.

0.0sThumb on the screen~2.0s
✓FIRST · ~0.5s
Is this about me?
If the answer is no, the thumb keeps going. Nothing else gets read.
✓SECOND · ~1.0s
How does it make me feel?
Seen. Hopeful. Curious. A little worried. Feelings come before facts.
✓OVER TIME
Do I trust this person?
Built slowly, post after post. That’s why showing up matters.
Me first. Feelings second. Facts later. People buy homes with the heart, then use facts to feel good about it.
Yo primero. Sentimientos segundo. Datos después.
