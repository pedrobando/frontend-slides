# ManyChat Flow — Keyword "MARCA" (El Motor de Marca)

Reel: "Tu marca personal no es tu logo" · Lead magnet: `El Motor de Marca` PDF via Google Drive link.
Flow shape (as requested): **Greet → Follow gate → Deliver link.**

---

## 0. Before you build the flow — Google Drive link

1. Upload `El Motor de Marca - Pedro Bando Rivera.pdf` to Google Drive.
2. Right-click → Share → General access: **Anyone with the link · Viewer**.
3. Copy the link and paste it wherever you see `[LINK-DRIVE]` below.

## 1. Trigger setup in ManyChat

1. ManyChat → **Automation → New Automation** (or use the "Comments" Instagram template).
2. Trigger: **Instagram → User comments on your Post or Reel**.
3. Select **the specific reel** (recommended — keeps stats clean) and set the trigger word: `MARCA`. Turn on "including similar words" so marca / Marca / MARCA! all fire.
4. Turn ON **Public Reply to comment** and add 3–4 variants so IG doesn't flag repetition (see §2).
5. Then the DM sequence (§3–§5).

## 2. Public comment replies (rotating variants)

- `¡Listo! 🔥 Revisa tu DM 📩`
- `Te lo acabo de enviar por DM 📩`
- `¡Dale! Mira tus mensajes 🙌`
- `Enviado ✅ Chequea tu inbox 📩`

**Button:** `✅ Ya te sigo` → continues to DM #3.

> **Strict enforcement (optional):** if your ManyChat plan shows the Instagram condition **"Is Follower"** (Flow → Condition step → Instagram account follower), put a Condition after the button:
> - **Is follower = Yes** → DM #3.
> - **Is follower = No** → message: `¡Casi! 😅 No me aparece que me sigas todavía. Dale follow a @pedrobandorivera y toca el botón otra vez.` → button `✅ Listo, ya te sigo` → back to the Condition.
> If your plan doesn't have that condition, the honor-system button above works fine.
