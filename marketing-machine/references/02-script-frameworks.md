# Stage 2 — Video Scripts (the 6 frameworks)

> The avatar told you **what to say**. The framework tells you **how to say it**.
> *Same avatar. Same pain. Different angle. Pick the framework that matches where your **audience**
> is in the journey — not where you want them to be.*

## Pick the framework by audience temperature

| Framework | Best for | Tone | Funnel stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AIDA** | Cold audiences, first impressions | Bold, attention-grabbing | Top |
| **PAS** | Pain-aware (warm) audiences | Empathetic, confrontational | Mid |
| **FAB** | Product-aware, comparing options | Clear, logical | Mid–Bottom |
| **SPIN** | 1-on-1 conversations, DMs | Consultative | Mid |
| **SCIPAB** | Long-form: emails, scripts, decks | Thorough, persuasive | Full funnel |
| **IDCA** | Retargeting, warm followers | Familiar, proof-driven | Bottom |

**Funnel mapping for the Meta machine:** Cold = **AIDA** · Warm = **PAS** or **FAB** · Retarget =
**IDCA**. SPIN is for DMs/coffee chats; SCIPAB for long-form (emails, landing pages, pitch decks).

How to use: confirm the avatar (Stage 1), ask which audience/video this is for, pick the framework,
then write the script in EN + ES. The examples below use a producing-agent recruiting avatar — swap
in the user's avatar and offer.

---

## AIDA — Attention · Interest · Desire · Action  *(cold / top of funnel)*

Stop the scroll → build curiosity → create want → drive the step.

**EN**
- **Attention:** "You closed 8 deals this year. Your broker collected their split. And you still
  don't know where deal 9 is coming from."
- **Interest:** "Most agents producing $1M–$3M are one system away from scaling. But nobody ever
  sits down and builds it with them."
- **Desire:** "At reThought, agents train live every Tuesday — marketing, production, prospecting,
  analytics. With a broker who picks up the phone. In two markets."
- **Action:** "Book 20 minutes with Pedro. No pitch. No pressure. Just an honest conversation about
  where you're going."

**ES**
- **Atención:** "Cerraste 8 deals este año. Tu broker cobró su split. Y todavía no sabes de dónde
  viene el deal número 9."
- **Interés:** "La mayoría de los agentes produciendo $1M a $3M están a un sistema de escalar. Pero
  nadie se sienta y lo construye con ellos."
- **Deseo:** "En reThought, los agentes entrenan en vivo cada martes — marketing, producción,
  prospecting, analytics. Con un broker que contesta el teléfono. En dos mercados."
- **Acción:** "Agenda 20 minutos con Pedro. Sin pitch. Sin presión. Solo una conversación real sobre
  a dónde vas."

## PAS — Problem · Agitate · Solution  *(warm / pain-aware)*

Name the problem → twist the knife (accurate, not mean) → offer relief.

**EN**
- **Problem:** "You're producing $1M–$3M a year and you're still winging it every single month."
- **Agitate:** "No pipeline system. No real training. A broker who collects splits and disappears.
  You're working harder than ever and you still can't predict where next month's check is coming
  from."
- **Solution:** "reThought was built for exactly this. Live weekly training. A 48-week curriculum. A
  broker who actually picks up the phone. Two markets. One community."

**ES**
- **Problema:** "Estás produciendo $1M a $3M al año y todavía estás improvisando cada mes."
- **Agitar:** "Sin sistema de pipeline. Sin training real. Un broker que cobra splits y desaparece.
  Estás trabajando más duro que nunca y todavía no puedes predecir de dónde viene el cheque del
  próximo mes."
- **Solución:** "reThought fue construido exactamente para esto. Training en vivo cada semana. Un
  currículo de 48 semanas. Un broker que de verdad contesta el teléfono. Dos mercados. Una
  comunidad."

> **On Agitate:** *"This is where most agents pull back because they feel mean. Don't. If you're
> truly describing your avatar's reality, it's not mean — it's accurate. And accuracy is what
> builds trust."*

## FAB — Feature · Advantage · Benefit  *(product-aware / comparing)*

What it is → why it matters vs. alternatives → what it means for THEM emotionally.

**EN**
- **Feature:** "reThought runs live training every Tuesday — marketing, production, prospecting, and
  analytics."
- **Advantage:** "Unlike pre-recorded courses or monthly Zoom calls with 200 people, this is a
  small-group session where you get direct feedback on YOUR business."
- **Benefit:** "You stop guessing what to do next. You show up Tuesday, you leave with a plan, and
  you execute the rest of the week with clarity."

**ES**
- **Característica:** "reThought corre training en vivo cada martes — marketing, producción,
  prospecting, y analytics."
- **Ventaja:** "A diferencia de cursos pre-grabados o Zoom calls mensuales con 200 personas, esto es
  una sesión de grupo pequeño donde recibes feedback directo sobre TU negocio."
- **Beneficio:** "Dejas de adivinar qué hacer después. Llegas el martes, te vas con un plan, y
  ejecutas el resto de la semana con claridad."

## SPIN — Situation · Problem · Implication · Need-Payoff  *(DMs / 1-on-1)*

Set context → name the friction → show cost of inaction → make the solution inevitable.

**EN**
- **Situation:** "You closed 8 deals last year at a $2M volume per deal."
- **Problem:** "But you don't have a repeatable pipeline. Every deal feels like you're starting from
  scratch."
- **Implication:** "If nothing changes, you'll close 8 again next year. Maybe 6. And you'll burn out
  doing it because the hustle doesn't scale."
- **Need-Payoff:** "What if you had a system that generated 3–5 qualified conversations per week —
  without cold calling? That's what we build at reThought."

**ES**
- **Situación:** "Cerraste 8 deals el año pasado a un volumen de $2M por deal."
- **Problema:** "Pero no tienes un pipeline repetible. Cada deal se siente como que estás empezando
  de cero."
- **Implicación:** "Si nada cambia, vas a cerrar 8 otra vez el año que viene. Quizás 6. Y te vas a
  quemar haciéndolo porque el hustle no escala."
- **Necesidad:** "¿Qué tal si tuvieras un sistema que generara 3 a 5 conversaciones calificadas por
  semana — sin cold calling? Eso es lo que construimos en reThought."

## SCIPAB — Situation · Complication · Implication · Position · Action · Benefit  *(long-form)*

The most thorough framework. Use for emails, landing pages, full video scripts, pitch decks.

**EN**
- **Situation:** "You're a producing agent. $1M–$3M in annual volume. You're not new — you've got
  skills."
- **Complication:** "But your brokerage treats you like a number. No real training. No marketing
  help. No accountability."
- **Implication:** "Without a system, you'll keep grinding at the same level. In 2 years, you're
  either out or back at a desk job."
- **Position:** "reThought is a production-centric boutique firm. We don't recruit 200 agents. We
  develop 20 — deeply."
- **Action:** "Book a 20-minute call. See the training calendar. Meet the community."
- **Benefit:** "You leave the call with clarity — whether you join or not."

**ES**
- **Situación:** "Eres un agente productivo. $1M a $3M en volumen anual. No eres nuevo — tienes
  skills."
- **Complicación:** "Pero tu brokerage te trata como un número. Sin training real. Sin ayuda de
  marketing. Sin accountability."
- **Implicación:** "Sin un sistema, vas a seguir moliendo al mismo nivel. En 2 años, estás afuera o
  de vuelta en un escritorio."
- **Posición:** "reThought es una firma boutique centrada en producción. No reclutamos 200 agentes.
  Desarrollamos 20 — profundamente."
- **Acción:** "Agenda una llamada de 20 minutos. Ve el calendario de training. Conoce la comunidad."
- **Beneficio:** "Sales de la llamada con claridad — seas que te unas o no."

## IDCA — Interest · Desire · Conviction · Action  *(retargeting / warm followers)*

**Skips Attention** — they already know you. They don't need to be stopped; they need to be
**convinced**. Acknowledge the relationship → paint what they want → prove it with numbers → push.

**EN**
- **Interest:** "You've been watching our training clips. You've seen our agents post their wins.
  Something about it keeps pulling you back."
- **Desire:** "You want what they have — a team that actually trains together, a broker who builds
  alongside you, and a system that doesn't depend on luck."
- **Conviction:** "Last quarter, our agents averaged 3 closings each. Not because they're
  superhuman — because they follow a system. The same system you'd get on day one."
- **Action:** "Stop watching from the sideline. Book 20 minutes. Let's see if this is the right
  fit."

**ES**
- **Interés:** "Has estado viendo nuestros clips de training. Has visto a nuestros agentes postear
  sus wins. Algo de esto te sigue jalando."
- **Deseo:** "Quieres lo que ellos tienen — un equipo que de verdad entrena junto, un broker que
  construye a tu lado, y un sistema que no depende de la suerte."
- **Convicción:** "El trimestre pasado, nuestros agentes promediaron 3 cierres cada uno. No porque
  sean superhéroes — porque siguen un sistema. El mismo sistema al que tendrías acceso desde el día
  uno."
- **Acción:** "Deja de mirar desde la banca. Agenda 20 minutos. Veamos si esto es el fit correcto."
