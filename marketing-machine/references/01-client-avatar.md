# Stage 1 — Client Avatar

> The avatar tells you **what to say**. Every other stage depends on it. Build this first, always.
> *Garbage avatar = garbage hooks, garbage ads, garbage everything.*

## "Feel It Before You Write It" — the 3 questions

Don't ask the user for demographics first. Demographics don't stop a scroll — *emotion* does. Ask
these three, in order, and pull their answers in the avatar's own words:

**Q1 — What is your avatar doing at 11pm that they shouldn't have to be doing?**
*(the pain, made concrete)*
> e.g. "Refreshing email waiting for a deal that never came. Wondering where the next commission
> check is coming from."

**Q2 — What have they tried that didn't work?**
*(the journey of frustration)*
> e.g. "KW training they stopped attending. eXp Zoom calls nobody showed up for. Un broker que
> nunca contestaba el teléfono."

**Q3 — What are they afraid to admit out loud?** ← THE GOLD
> e.g. "I'm almost 30 and I still don't know if real estate is actually going to work for me."

**Then:** *"Write from Question 3. That's the hook. That sentence they're afraid to say out loud —
that's what stops the scroll."*
*ES: "Escriban desde la pregunta 3. Eso es el hook. Esa oración que les da miedo decir en voz alta —
eso es lo que para el scroll."*

## How to run it

1. Ask the three questions (conversationally — one at a time if the user is engaged).
2. If answers are vague, push for the concrete, specific, slightly-uncomfortable version. "Wondering
   where the next check comes from" beats "feeling stressed."
3. Identify the **niche** behind the answers (e.g. producing agents at $1M–$3M; first-time Latino
   buyers relocating to Florida; VA-loan-eligible veterans). The tighter, the better.
4. Produce the avatar profile below.

## Avatar profile (the artifact)

```
AVATAR: <name the avatar, e.g. "Producing-but-stuck Pedro">
Market / language: <city/region · EN / ES / both>

THE PAIN (Q1):        <11pm, in their words>
WHAT THEY'VE TRIED (Q2): <failed attempts, in their words>
THE SECRET FEAR (Q3): <what they won't say out loud — THE HOOK SEED>

What they actually want: <the outcome under the fear>
What makes me their guide: <the specific complexity only this agent provides>
```

## The 5-Pillar Frame (for every outbound contact)

Once the avatar is set, every call/DM/voicemail follows these five pillars. This is what turns a
cold touch into a guide relationship:

1. **Who I am** — name, brokerage, how we met / where they found me.
2. **Why I'm calling** — the specific benefit, not "just checking in."
3. **What you're looking for** — acknowledge their keyword / stated need back to them.
4. **I can help you get it** — reference the actual process or service.
5. **The specific thing ONLY I can provide** — a market brief, a CMA, a zoning analysis, a
   relocation plan. *This pillar is non-negotiable — it's what adds complexity and makes you a
   guide instead of a door-opener.*

## AI shortcut — hook extraction (only once the avatar is locked)

When you need hook candidates fast, run this against the finished avatar profile:

```
Using my Avatar doc, extract 5 candidate hook sentences that could stop the scroll in a Meta ad.
Rules: second person. Short. Numeral-first when possible. Names the pain in their words.
No "revolutionary / game-changing / disruptive."
Return 5. Then pick the one that hurts the most and explain why in one sentence.
```
