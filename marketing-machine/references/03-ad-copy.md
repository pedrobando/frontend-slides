# Stage 3 — Ad Copy

> The script is the angle. The ad copy is that angle compressed for the feed, written so it **does
> its own targeting**. Under Special Ad Category you can't target by age, gender, interest, or tight
> zip — so the words have to filter the right person in and the wrong person out.

## The one rule: specificity is the targeting

*Generic fails. Specific filters.*

- ❌ "Calling all real estate professionals" → targets no one, filters no one.
- ✅ "Milwaukee agents, 23–35, producing $1M–$3M who are tired of carrying a broker's split" →
  the right person feels *seen*; everyone else scrolls past. That self-selection **is** your
  targeting.
- ✅ "Relocating your family to Lake Nona? The school zoning just changed for 2026." → only the
  relocating-to-Lake-Nona parent stops.

Pull the specifics straight from the avatar (Stage 1): the 11pm pain, the failed attempts, the
secret fear, the exact city/price band/situation.

## Structure of a piece of ad copy

1. **Hook (line 1)** — the scroll-stopper. Second person, short, numeral-first when possible, names
   the pain in their words. *Banned: revolutionary, game-changing, disruptive.* This is the Q3 fear
   from the avatar, sharpened. (Use the hook-extraction prompt in `01-client-avatar.md`.)
2. **Body** — run the framework that matches the audience temperature (see `02-script-frameworks.md`):
   cold→AIDA, warm→PAS/FAB, retarget→IDCA. Keep it tight; the feed is not a landing page.
3. **CTA + keyword** — one clear next step, and the **comment keyword** that fires ManyChat
   (e.g. *"Comment SYSTEMS and I'll send you the 48-week curriculum"*). The keyword in the caption
   **must exactly match** the ManyChat trigger you set in Stage 6.

## Writing copy per funnel stage

- **Cold (AIDA, objective = Engagement):** end on a comment keyword so ManyChat can fire and Meta
  learns to find commenters. *"Comment SYSTEMS."*
- **Warm (PAS or FAB, objective = Leads):** can push to a form or Calendly; a comparison keyword
  like `COMPARE` works well for FAB.
- **Retarget (IDCA, objective = Conversions):** dual path — *"Book in my bio, or comment NOW and I'll
  send the link."* They already know you; reduce friction to the booking.

## Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Write to ONE person (the avatar) | Write to "everyone who might buy a house" |
| Use their exact words, city, price band | Use agency jargon or hype adjectives |
| Let the copy filter the audience | Rely on demographic targeting (you can't) |
| End cold ads on a comment keyword | Forget to wire ManyChat before publishing |
| Match keyword to caption to trigger | Change the keyword and forget the caption |

→ Copy approved? Place it in **Stage 4 — Meta Funnel** (`04-meta-funnel.md`) and make sure its
keyword matches your **Stage 6 — ManyChat** build.
