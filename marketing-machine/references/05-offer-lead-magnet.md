# Stage 5 — The Offer / Lead Magnet

> *"A lead magnet is a specific, valuable piece of content your avatar receives in exchange for
> their information — that delivers immediately on the promise your hook made."*
> Deliverable: a finished offer built from `assets/offer-template.html` (PDF-ready HTML).

## The golden rule

**The lead magnet must solve the EXACT pain your hook promised — nothing more, nothing less.**
If the hook says *"7 steps from renting to owning,"* the lead magnet is about 7 steps from renting
to owning. If the hook says *"before you buy on Zillow, read this,"* it's about exactly that.

> *"An ad that promises something and delivers nothing is worse than no ad at all — it builds
> distrust before the relationship starts."*

A good offer also **demonstrates complexity** — it shows the agent knows things the prospect
doesn't, which is what turns them into a guide (not just a door-opener), and leads naturally to the
next step.

## Good offer types

- One-page briefs (HOA rules, CDD fees, school-zoning changes, a neighborhood market brief)
- Automated/recurring market reports (set in the CRM, monthly or weekly)
- Buyer / seller guides, bilingual closing-document explainers
- CMA templates
- Case studies with real photos

## Structure (the proven 7-part lead magnet)

Modeled on the finished lead magnets in this repo (e.g. Kermit's *"Antes de Zillow"*). The
`offer-template.html` follows this exactly:

1. **Cover** — the hook as a headline + a one-line subhead naming the payoff + agent photo &
   credentials.
2. **Letter** — a short personal narrative that builds trust ("I started where you are…") + an
   agent sidecar (years, bilingual, specializations).
3. **Main content** — the core value, usually a numbered list (e.g. *7 costly mistakes*), each item:
   headline · what the mistake is · a short tag/category.
4. **Checklist** — an actionable framework, often a memorable acronym. Example from Kermit, the
   **CASA** checklist: **C**laridad (clarity of intent) · **A**nálisis (financial analysis) ·
   **S**istema (team setup) · **A**cción (action / signing). Checkboxes under each.
5. **Closing line** — one powerful reframe of the relationship.
   *e.g. "No vengo a venderte una casa. Vengo a ayudarte a no comprar la equivocada."*
6. **CTA** — photo + one specific next step ("let's do the short call") + contact info.
7. **Brand close** — logo / sign-off.

## The bridge — 3 messages between form-fill and CRM handoff

The offer isn't the end; it warms the lead from *"I clicked a thing"* to *"this person delivered
what they promised"* before your CRM smart-plans take over.

- **Message 1 — Instant delivery (0–5 min, email + text):** send the lead magnet immediately.
  *e.g. "Hey [First Name] — it's [Your Name] from reThought. Your [Lead Magnet Name] just hit your
  inbox. Let me know if you have any questions."*
- **Message 2 — Value reinforcement (24h, email):** don't repeat the magnet — **expand around it**.
  One insight, one stat, or one story that makes it more valuable.
- **Message 3 — Qualifying question (48–72h, text):** one question that reveals their situation and
  invites a reply.

## How to build it with the user

1. Confirm the hook (from the avatar / ad). The offer's promise = the hook's promise.
2. Outline the 3–5 content sections (or the numbered list).
3. Draft a memorable checklist/acronym for part 4.
4. Write the closing reframe line and the CTA.
5. Fill `assets/offer-template.html` — replace every `[[PLACEHOLDER]]`. Keep it bilingual if the
   avatar is. It's print/PDF-ready (open in a browser → Print → Save as PDF).
6. Draft the 3 bridge messages.
