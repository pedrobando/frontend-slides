# Florida forms and law changes, 2025-2026

Research notes for the Florida half of the reThought Real Estate training deck (Orlando agents, Stellar MLS). Current as of Oct 27, 2026. Items dated before 2025 are tagged [BACKGROUND]. Items that rest on one secondary source or could not be checked against a primary source are tagged [UNCONFIRMED] or [SECONDARY].

Short version for the Wisconsin comparison. Florida has no state law like Wisconsin Act 69. The Florida rules on private listings come from NAR policy and Stellar MLS rules, and the paperwork lives in two places: the Florida Realtors listing agreement (paragraph 6(b) seller opt-out, paragraph 10 buyer-broker compensation) and Stellar's "MLS Options for Property Owners - Authorization" form. Buyer-broker compensation travels on separate forms (CASSB-1, Riders FF and GG), never in the MLS.

| Wisconsin item in the deck | Closest Florida equivalent | Status |
|---|---|---|
| New WB listing contracts with marketing duties | Florida Realtors ERS listing agreements revised Sept/Oct 2025 (ERS-21tb etc.) and EBLA-13 Jan 5, 2026. Paragraph 5 now says seller and broker follow local MLS policy and sign any needed MLS forms | In use now |
| Seller "Public Marketing Opt-Out" form (state-prescribed) | Stellar MLS "MLS Options for Property Owners - Authorization" (effective 9/16/2025, revised 5/2026). Plus listing agreement paragraph 6(b) "Not Publicly Market to the Public/Seller Opt-Out" | MLS rule, not state law |
| Compensation section in offers | FR/Bar contract Riders FF and GG (checkboxes in paragraph 19 since the 12/24 revision); CASSB-1 separate compensation agreement (Jan 5, 2026) | In use now |
| Mandatory date (WI Jan 1, 2027) | No Florida statutory deadline. Stellar deadlines started Sept 16 and Sept 30, 2025 | n/a |

## 1. Florida Realtors forms: listing, buyer brokerage, compensation, seller disclosure

### January 5, 2026 release (the big one)

Florida Realtors released new and revised listing, leasing and compensation forms that became effective Jan 5, 2026, on Form Simplicity at 9 a.m. [Source: Florida Realtors, "Florida Realtors Rolls Out New, Updated Forms," Amy Connolly, Jan 2, 2026, https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/01/florida-realtors-rolls-out-new-updated-forms]

New forms:
- CASSB-1, Compensation Agreement - Seller or Seller's Broker to Buyer's Broker (Rev 1/26). Merges the 2024 CASB-1 (seller to buyer's broker) and CABB-1 (seller's broker to buyer's broker).
- MCSB-1, Modification to Compensation Agreement - Seller or Seller's Broker to Buyer's Broker. Replaces MCA-1 and MCA-2.

Retired and removed from Form Simplicity: CASB-1, CABB-1, MCA-1, MCA-2. Deals already signed on the old forms stay valid and enforceable. [Source: Florida Realtors, "Understanding the Latest Compensation Form Update," Heather Rhodes, Associate General Counsel, Jan 28, 2026, https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/01/understanding-latest-compensation-form-update]

What changed in CASSB-1 (same Jan 28, 2026 source; form text read from https://www.floridarealtors.org/sites/default/files/2025-12/Compensation%20Agreement%20-%20Seller%20or%20Sellers%20Broker%20to%20Buyers%20Broker_CASSB-1.pdf):
- Paragraph 1 has two checkboxes for who is offering compensation, seller or seller's broker. There is no default. Florida Realtors warns that leaving both blank could create enforceability problems.
- Paragraph 2 says whether the property is listed by the seller's broker or not listed. A seller who signed only the Commission Agreement checks "not listed."
- Paragraph 4 default term dropped from 30 days to 15 days if the blank is left empty.
- Paragraph 6 dispute resolution depends on the payer. Seller pays, then mediation then litigation, or arbitration if chosen. Seller's broker pays, then binding arbitration under the NAR Code of Ethics.

Revised forms in the same release:
- EBLA-13 Exclusive Brokerage Listing Agreement (Rev 1/2026). Adds the NAR Multiple Listing Options for Sellers policy. Paragraph 10 buyer-broker compensation changed from "check all that apply" to "check one option." If nothing is checked, option (c) "No compensation will be offered" applies. [Redline: https://www.floridarealtors.org/sites/default/files/2025-12/Exclusive%20Brokerage%20Listing%20Agreement_EBLA-13_redlined_1.pdf]
- LSLA-5 Limited Service Listing Agreement, VLLA-6 Vacant Land Listing Agreement, ERS-10cp commercial listing, ERL-14 and ERLC-2 lease listings. All add the NAR MLS options policy. ERLC-2 also drops the repealed business rent tax.
- EBBA-8sa, EBBA-8tb, EBBA-8tn, EBBA-8nr Exclusive Buyer Brokerage Agreements (Rev 1/26, replacing EBBA-7 Rev 7/24). New buyer acknowledgment that owners may have audio recording or video surveillance (doorbell cameras etc.). Removed language letting the broker run a credit check.
- SA-5 Showing Agreement. Credit-check language removed.
- VAC-15 Vacant Land Contract. Calendar days, bifurcation language removed.
- CL-11 Contract to Lease. Signing-deposit options.
- RCD-8 Receipt of Condominium/Cooperative Documents. Adds turnover inspection reports.
- MISIRS-2 Milestone Inspection and Structural Integrity Reserve Study Disclosure. Adds association information and a notice that the seller completes it.
- MDSTS-2 Miami-Dade Septic Tank System Disclosure (not relevant to Orlando).

### How buyer-broker compensation is documented in Florida now

1. Listing agreement paragraph 10 (ERS-20sa/21tb/21tn/18nr, EBLA-13). Seller authorizes broker-paid, seller-paid, or no offer. Check one.
2. Separate compensation agreement CASSB-1 (seller or seller's broker to buyer's broker). Required for option (a) or (b). Modify with MCSB-1.
3. Buyer brokerage agreement EBBA-8 paragraph 7. Buyer agrees to a fixed dollar amount or percentage. Money the broker receives from the owner or owner's broker reduces what the buyer owes. Paragraph 14 says the broker "may not receive compensation from any source that exceeds the amount or rate agreed to with Consumer." [EBBA-8tb redline, https://www.floridarealtors.org/sites/default/files/2025-12/Exclusive%20Buyer%20Brokerage%20Agreement_Transaction%20Broker_EBBA-8tb_redlined_0.pdf]
4. In the offer. FR/Bar contract Rider FF (Credit Related to Buyer's Broker Compensation, seller credit the buyer applies to the buyer's broker) and Rider GG (Seller's Agreement with Respect to Buyer's Broker Compensation, makes the contract contingent on a signed compensation agreement; 3-day default; buyer may terminate and recover deposit). Released Sept 9, 2024. [BACKGROUND, https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2024/09/florida-realtors-introduces-new-contract-riders] Since the 12/24 FR/Bar revision, paragraph 19 has checkboxes for FF and GG. For the CRSP17 contract the counterpart is CARB-1 (Nov 2024). [https://www.floridarealtors.org/law-ethics/nar-settlement-sitzer-burnett-lawsuit]
5. Never in the MLS. The NAR settlement practice changes of Aug 17, 2024 bar offers of compensation in any MLS field, and every Florida Realtors listing form footer repeats this. [BACKGROUND]

### Stellar MLS: the seller form that keeps a listing off the MLS or delays it

This is the Florida analog of Wisconsin's opt-out form. It is an MLS form, not a state form.

- NAR adopted Multiple Listing Options for Sellers on Mar 25, 2025. MLSs had to implement it by Sept 30, 2025. It allows office exclusive and delayed marketing listings, each backed by a signed seller certification. Clear Cooperation (enter within 1 business day of public marketing) did not change. [https://www.nar.realtor/about-nar/policies/multiple-listing-options-for-sellers]
- Stellar launched Delayed Distribution and the new Office Exclusive rules on Sept 16, 2025. Office Exclusive and Temporary Exclusion listings had to be fully entered in Matrix, with the new form on file, by Sept 30, 2025. [https://www.stellarmls.com/DelayedDistribution, "Last Updated: October 2025"]
- Form name: "MLS Options for Property Owners - Authorization." First version effective 9/16/2025 (approved 8-15-2025). Current version "Revised 5/2026," posted on Stellar's Coming Soon page as "Multiple Options for Sellers June 2026." Hosted on Stellar's site CDN. [https://irp.cdn-website.com/3d0f9886/files/uploaded/Multiple+Options+for+Sellers+June+2026.pdf]
- The 5/2026 form makes the seller pick one of four options:
  - Coming Soon. Up to 14 calendar days, then auto-Active on day 15. Must be entered within 5 business days of the listing start or 1 business day of marketing. All marketing must say "Coming Soon." Seller marks whether showings are allowed. New in the 5/2026 version.
  - Delayed Distribution. Active in the MLS and visible to all Stellar agents, but off IDX and public sites for 5 calendar days from the listing start. Cannot be extended.
  - Office Exclusive. Entered in Stellar but visible only to the listing brokerage. No public marketing.
  - Office Exclusive with Temporary Exclusion. Visible only to the listing broker and agent until a set date, then reverts to Office Exclusive.
- Every owner initials a fair housing affirmation and an "MLS Benefits Waiver." All owners, the broker or office manager, and the agent must sign. No strikethroughs accepted. Office Exclusive sellers initial that "all social media platforms public or private," email and text blasts, and multi-brokerage sharing networks count as public marketing.
- Stellar's Coming Soon page (published Oct 22, 2026) says the status is "launching soon." Go-live date [UNCONFIRMED]. Coming Soon listings must have the form; days on market do not accumulate; offers allowed but the listing must go Active before Pending; form must be produced within 2 business days of an MLS request. [https://www.stellarmls.com/coming-soon-listings]
- Deadlines and fines. Enter a listing within 5 business days of a signed listing agreement, or 1 business day after any public marketing. A Clear Cooperation violation is a Level 3 fine charged to the agent, not the broker: $500 first offense, $2,500 second, then a hearing with up to $15,000 and possible loss of MLS access. [https://www.stellarmls.com/clearcooperation]

## 2. FR/Bar residential contract (standard and AS IS)

- Rev 12/24 (FloridaBar-7 and ASIS-7), both forms changed the same way [BACKGROUND, Dec 2024]. Paragraph 9 defines "Closing Services" by reference to s. 627.7711(1)(a) and makes each party pay its own closing services. Paragraph 15(a) drops the cooperating broker's right to ask for half of a listing broker's share of a retained deposit after buyer default. Paragraph 19 adds checkboxes for Riders FF and GG. [SECONDARY: Miami Realtors "Roadmap of Florida Forms," updated Feb 5, 2025, https://www.miamirealtors.com/wp-content/uploads/bsk-pdf-manager/2025/02/02-06-2025-Roadmap-of-Florida-Forms-Dec-24-Jan-25-New-Florida-Realtors-Forms.pdf. The old FinCEN GTO paragraph is replaced by a "FinCEN Real Estate Reporting Obligation" for non-financed residential transfers to an entity or trust "beginning March 1, 2026." If a report is required, both parties give the closing agent beneficial-owner data no later than the day before closing, and the buyer pays the closing agent's fee to prepare and file it. [Florida Realtors, Feb 26, 2026, URL above; redline read directly, https://www.floridarealtors.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/AS%20IS%20Residential%20Contract%20for%20Sale%20and%20Purchase%20(FloridaRealtors-FloridaBar-ASIS-7x)_Redlined[1].pdf]

FinCEN and DOJ have appealed. Two other federal judges upheld the rule. The contract paragraph is conditional ("If this transaction requires completion of a FinCEN Report"), so it sits dormant for now. [FinCEN RRE FAQs, https://www.fincen.gov/rre-faqs, status items dated through May 18, 2026; appeal still pending in a Sept 2026 secondary update, https://www.aide-re.com/post/fincen-residential-real-estate-rule-update-september-2026, SECONDARY]
- Condo rider. CR-7 Rider A updated July 1, 2025 for the 7-day review period (section 4).
- No other FR/Bar revision found between Feb 27 and Oct 27, 2026.

[https://www.flhouse.gov/Statutes/2026/Chapter0475/All/, checked Oct 27, 2026]

- Bills that failed:
  - 2025 SB 1770 "Duties of a Real Estate Licensee" (would have revised s. 475.278 duties and defined "limited confidentiality") and House companion HB 805. Both died June 16, 2025. [https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2025/1770]

## 4. Seller disclosure duties

### Flood disclosure, s. 689.302

- [BACKGROUND] Created by ch. 2024-215 (CS/CS/HB 1049). Approved May 29, 2024. Effective Oct 1, 2024. Original form had two items: flood insurance claim filed (including NFIP), and federal flood assistance received (including FEMA). [https://laws.flrules.org/2024/215; https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2024/1049]
- SB 948 (ch. 2025-166). Approved June 20, 2025. Effective Oct 1, 2025. Passed 37-0 and 114-0. [https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2025/948; enrolled text read directly]
- What the seller must do now. Complete and give the buyer a "FLOOD DISCLOSURE" in the statutory form "at or before the time the sales contract is executed," for any residential real property. The form has:
  - Flood insurance warning. Homeowners' policies do not cover flood. Buyer should talk to an insurance agent.
  - (1) NEW in 2025. Seller has / has no knowledge of any flooding that damaged the property during the seller's ownership.
  - (2) Seller has / has not filed a flood damage insurance claim, including with NFIP.
  - (3) Seller has / has not received federal assistance for flood damage, including FEMA.
  - (4) Definition of "flooding": overflow of inland or tidal waters, unusual and rapid runoff from an established water source such as a river, stream or drainage ditch, or sustained standing water from rainfall.
- Same bill also requires flood disclosure from landlords (new s. 83.512, leases of 1 year or longer, separate document; tenant may terminate after substantial loss if the landlord lied), condo and co-op developers (s. 718.503(1), 719.503), and mobile home park owners (s. 723.011).
- No 2026 amendment. 2026 statute history shows only ch. 2024-215 and ch. 2025-166. [https://www.flhouse.gov/Statutes/2026/689.302/]
- The statute does not state a penalty for sellers who skip it. [Remedy UNCONFIRMED]
- Forms: FD-2 (sales), LFD-1 (leases), Sept 30, 2025.

### Condo sales, HB 913 (2025)

- CS/CS/HB 913, ch. 2025-175. Governor approval 6/23/2025 per the Senate bill page (one news report says June 18). Effective July 1, 2025. [https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2025/913; enrolled text read directly]
- Resale contracts, s. 718.503(2)(d) and (e). The buyer's window went from 3 to 7 days (excluding weekends and legal holidays). Applies to the governing documents (declaration, articles, bylaws and rules, most recent annual financial statement and budget, FAQ document) and, separately, to the milestone inspection summary, turnover inspection report and most recent SIRS. Buyer can void within 7 days after signing and receiving the documents, or extend closing up to 7 days. Waivers are void. Right ends at closing. Developer sales keep 15 days.
- What the seller must deliver at the seller's expense, s. 718.503(2)(a) as of 2026: declaration, articles, bylaws and rules, annual financial statement and budget, inspector-prepared milestone summary if applicable, most recent SIRS or a statement that none was done, turnover inspection report (turnovers on or after July 1, 2023), and the FAQ document. The buyer may also get the DBPR governance form. A licensee who provides or obtains these documents is not liable for errors in them (s. 718.503(2)(c)). [https://www.flhouse.gov/Statutes/2026/718.503/]
- [BACKGROUND, ch. 2024-244] Contracts signed after Dec 31, 2024 must state in conspicuous type whether the association must have a milestone inspection, turnover report or SIRS and whether it has one.
- Milestone inspections now apply to buildings three habitable stories or more (was three stories). SIRS deadline extended to Dec 31, 2025. Associations must post 12 months of approved board minutes online. [Florida Realtors, June 25, 2025, URL above]
- Forms: CR-7 Rider A, CRSP17x_F, COOP-4 (July 1, 2025); RCD-8 and MISIRS-2 (Jan 5, 2026).

### 2026 condo laws

- None of substance. HB 657/SB 1498 (HOA dissolution, condo courts) failed. HB 255/SB 638 (turnover certificate database) failed. [Florida Realtors 2026 final report; Mosaic HOA recap Apr 29, 2026, https://mosaichoa.com/blog/florida-hoa-2026-legislative-session-recap/, SECONDARY]
- The only 2026 change to s. 718.503 is the reviser's bill renumbering (ch. 2026-14), which is why Addendum F got a 7/2026 date.

## 5. Other items in the next 60-90 days (confirmed only)

- Amendment 3 (CS/HJR 1-F) is on the Nov 3, 2026 ballot. Needs 60%. If it passes, effective Jan 1, 2027. Non-school homestead exemption rises to $150,000 on Jan 1, 2027 and $250,000 on Jan 1, 2028. Non-homestead assessment cap drops from 10% to 5%. School taxes, Save Our Homes and portability unchanged. Extra residency requirements for new residents after Jan 1, 2027. First affected tax bills Nov 2027. [St. Johns County Property Appraiser, https://www.sjcpa.gov/amendment3/; bill page https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2026F/1F, passed June 2, 2026 (House 75-26, Senate 30-9), filed with Secretary of State June 16, 2026]
  - A Leon County circuit judge ordered the ballot title and summary rewritten in early Aug 2026. The amendment stayed on the ballot and the state did not appeal. [Fox 35 Orlando, Aug 7, 2026, https://www.fox35orlando.com/news/florida-rewrite-property-tax-amendment-ballot-language-after-judges-ruling]
  - Florida Realtors' board voted to support it, Aug 23, 2026. Public radio reported in Sept 2026 that the association is the main funder of the Vote Yes on 3 committee. Expect client questions, and check brokerage policy on what agents say about a ballot measure. [https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/08/breaking-news-florida-realtors-supports-property-tax-reform; WLRN, Sept 21, 2026, https://www.wlrn.org/light/government-politics/2026-09-21/florida-realtors-pour-millions-of-dollars-into-amendment-3-push-as-opponents-warn-of-budget-cuts]
- HB 7031-E (ch. 2026-239), approved June 29, 2026. New s. 689.261(3). [Enrolled text read directly; DOR bulletin PTO 26-15, July 13, 2026, https://floridarevenue.com/property/Documents/PTO_BUL_26-15.pdf]
  - Starting Feb 1, 2027, every public-facing online listing platform (websites, web apps, mobile apps; social media excluded) must show estimated property taxes for Florida residential listings. It may use the DOR formula at listing price and current millage, or the countywide average millage plus a link to the property appraiser's estimator, each with a disclaimer. A platform that does neither must hide the current owner's taxes and link to the appraiser.
