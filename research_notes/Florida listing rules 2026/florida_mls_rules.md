# Florida MLS rules on listing marketing, as of Oct 27, 2026

Scope: Florida only. Built for the Florida slides of the reThought Real Estate team training, whose Florida agents list in the Orlando area on Stellar MLS. Research date Oct 27, 2026.

How to read this file. Every fact has a source link and a date. "Primary" means Stellar's own rulebook, forms or pages, or an association's own release. [UNCONFIRMED] means I saw it only in a search snippet, on a member-gated or paywalled page, or in one secondary source. Anything dated before 2025 is labeled background.

The short version for slides. In 2026 Stellar added a fourth listing option, Coming Soon, capped at 14 calendar days. It rewrote its seller form in May 2026 so one form covers all four options. Fines did not change. Clear Cooperation is still enforced at 1 business day after public marketing. Stellar was one of seven MLSs Compass and Rocket called out by name in March 2026, but Stellar was not in the Compass demand letter or any lawsuit, and I found no public Florida MLS response to the ultimatum, the lawsuits or Open Real Estate.

## 1. Stellar MLS changes in 2026

### New: Coming Soon status, mid-2026

This is the biggest Florida change of the year. Stellar's Clear Cooperation and Delayed Distribution pages still say "Stellar MLS does not have a coming soon status" and that the board is "exploring" one. Those pages are stamped "Last Updated: October 2025" and are now out of date. The rulebook is current.

- **Rule text.** Article 05.11(E) of the Stellar rulebook now lists Coming Soon as an option. Page stamp "Last updated Oct 15, 2026." Primary. [Stellar Rules Art. 05.11](https://rules.stellarmls.com/article/article-0511-MLS-Listing-Options-for-Property-Owners)
- **Length.** Up to 14 calendar days. The system flips the listing to Active on day 15 if the agent has not. No extensions. A home not ready to show after 14 days goes to Temporarily Off Market. [Stellar Coming Soon page](https://www.stellarmls.com/coming-soon-listings), undated page, copyright 2026; [Art. 05.11](https://rules.stellarmls.com/article/article-0511-MLS-Listing-Options-for-Property-Owners), Oct 15, 2026
- **One use per listing cycle.** Once it leaves Coming Soon it can never go back, even if a pending deal falls apart. [Coming Soon page FAQ 3 and 10.4](https://www.stellarmls.com/coming-soon-listings)
- **Who sees it.** All Stellar agents and brokers. [Art. 05.11(E)](https://rules.stellarmls.com/article/article-0511-MLS-Listing-Options-for-Property-Owners)
- **Public marketing allowed, with a label.** Social media and yard signs are allowed if every piece says "Coming Soon" and does not present the home as Active. The listing must be in Stellar within 1 business day of any Coming Soon marketing. [Coming Soon page FAQ 9.1 to 9.4](https://www.stellarmls.com/coming-soon-listings)
- **Showings.** Allowed at the owner's choice, and cooperating agents may show too. Active listings must be showable. Coming Soon listings do not have to be. [Coming Soon page FAQ 6](https://www.stellarmls.com/coming-soon-listings); [Stellar news, July 6, 2026](https://www.stellarmls.com/more-ways-to-market-listings-with-stellar-mls)
- **Offers.** Allowed. On an accepted offer the agent must move the listing to Active, then Pending, within 2 business days. It cannot jump straight to Pending. [Coming Soon page FAQ 7 and 10.3](https://www.stellarmls.com/coming-soon-listings)
- **Days on market and history.** ADOM and CDOM do not accrue during Coming Soon, though third-party sites may count them. Listing history does not show the Coming Soon period. Price history is not displayed. [Coming Soon page FAQ 8 and 11](https://www.stellarmls.com/coming-soon-listings)
- **IDX and portals.** May go to IDX and syndication, subject to the owner's distribution choices. [Art. 05.11(E)](https://rules.stellarmls.com/article/article-0511-MLS-Listing-Options-for-Property-Owners)
- **Photo.** At least one front exterior photo. [Coming Soon page](https://www.stellarmls.com/coming-soon-listings)
- **Fair housing.** Stellar says agents "must not use Coming Soon status to create selective access." [Coming Soon page](https://www.stellarmls.com/coming-soon-listings)
- **Clear Cooperation.** Stellar treats a Coming Soon listing entered in the MLS as compliant with Clear Cooperation. [Coming Soon page FAQ 14](https://www.stellarmls.com/coming-soon-listings)
- **Launch date. [UNCONFIRMED]** Stellar has not published a go-live date, and the Coming Soon page still says the status is "launching soon." The dated evidence brackets it to late May through early July 2026. The seller form carries "Revised 5/2026" and a "June 2026" file name ([form PDF](https://irp.cdn-website.com/3d0f9886/files/uploaded/Multiple+Options+for+Sellers+June+2026.pdf)). Stellar's English Coming Soon tutorial video was uploaded May 22, 2026 and the Spanish one June 5, 2026, per Vimeo metadata for the videos embedded on the Coming Soon page. A Stellar post dated July 6, 2026 lists Coming Soon among the options Stellar "supports" ([Stellar news](https://www.stellarmls.com/more-ways-to-market-listings-with-stellar-mls)). In a Sept 15, 2026 Inman op-ed, Stellar CEO Shayne Fairley wrote that Stellar offers "a coming-soon status" ([Inman](https://www.inman.com/2026/09/15/private-listings-mls-opinion/); paywalled, seen in search snippets only).
- My read. The timing lines up with the March 19, 2026 Compass and Rocket letter that called Stellar out by name (see question 3). No source says one caused the other, so do not put that on a slide as fact.

### Changed: one seller form for all four options, revised May 2026

- The new form is "MLS Options for Property Owners – Authorization," marked "Revised 5/2026." It replaces the version marked "Effective 9/16/2025," "#64212051 v24, Approved 8-15-2025." Primary. [May 2026 form](https://irp.cdn-website.com/3d0f9886/files/uploaded/Multiple+Options+for+Sellers+June+2026.pdf); [Sept 2025 form](https://irp.cdn-website.com/3d0f9886/files/uploaded/Multiple+Options+for+Sellers+Form.final.pdf)
- What changed on the form. It adds a Coming Soon box with a showings Yes/No line. It adds a note that the form "is only required if the property owner does not wish to immediately list the property as active." It splits the waiver so Coming Soon and Delayed Distribution sellers initial one paragraph, while Office Exclusive and Temporary Exclusion sellers initial the longer give-up-your-benefits paragraphs. Details are under question 2.

### Changed: deadline to produce the form

- Rule now. The broker must hand over the signed form within **2 business days** of an MLS request. That applies to Office Exclusive, Temporary Exclusion, Delayed Distribution and Coming Soon. Missing it is a Level II automatic fine. [Art. 05.11](https://rules.stellarmls.com/article/article-0511-MLS-Listing-Options-for-Property-Owners), Oct 15, 2026
- Older Stellar FAQ text still says "within 24 hours of request" for Delayed Distribution ([Delayed Distribution page, DD14](https://www.stellarmls.com/DelayedDistribution), Oct 2025). The rulebook controls. Teach 2 business days, and tell agents to keep the form ready anyway.

### Unchanged in 2026

- **Clear Cooperation.** Enter the listing within 5 business days of the owner's signature or the agreement's start date, whichever is later, or within 1 business day of any public marketing, whichever comes first. Public marketing while a listing sits as Office Exclusive or Temporary Exclusion means release to the full MLS within 1 business day. [Art. 05.01](https://rules.stellarmls.com/article/article-0501-submission-of-listings), page stamp Oct 26, 2026, rule text "(Updated 08/2025)"
- **Delayed Distribution** is still 5 calendar days, with no extension. The listing is Active and visible to all Stellar agents but kept off IDX and syndication during the delay. [Art. 05.11(D)](https://rules.stellarmls.com/article/article-0511-MLS-Listing-Options-for-Property-Owners), "(Updated 08/2025)"
- **Fines.** No 2026 change. Article 11.04 page stamp Sept 27, 2026. Primary. [Art. 11.04](https://rules.stellarmls.com/article/article-1104-penalties-for-inaccurate-or-incomplete-data)

| Violation | Level | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | After that |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Late entry or Clear Cooperation breach, Art. 05.01 | Level III Severe, automatic | $500 | $2,500 | hearing | hearing, up to $15,000, possible suspension or termination |
| Not producing the signed options form within 2 business days, Art. 05.11 | Level II, automatic | $500 | $1,000 | $5,000 | hearing, up to $15,000 |

- **Who pays.** "Fines are billed to the Subscriber/agent, the Participant is ultimately responsible." [Art. 11.04](https://rules.stellarmls.com/article/article-1104-penalties-for-inaccurate-or-incomplete-data). Unpaid after 30 days, the agent is suspended with a $250 reinstatement fee, and the invoice moves to the broker's account. If it stays unpaid, "your broker and all those in your office risk losing Stellar MLS access." [Stellar Fines & Violations FAQs](https://www.stellarmls.com/resources/rules-regulations/fines-and-violations-faqs), undated
- **How Stellar finds violations.** An automated scanner called Listing Data Checker, plus confidential agent reports. Stellar says it never reveals who reported. [Fines & Violations FAQs](https://www.stellarmls.com/resources/rules-regulations/fines-and-violations-faqs)

## 2. What the Stellar seller form requires

Form name: **"MLS Options for Property Owners – Authorization,"** Revised 5/2026. Stellar's pages also call it the "Multiple Listing Options for Sellers" form and, for Coming Soon, the "Stellar MLS-approved Coming Soon form." Same document. Primary. [Form PDF](https://irp.cdn-website.com/3d0f9886/files/uploaded/Multiple+Options+for+Sellers+June+2026.pdf)

**When it is needed.** Only when the seller does not want to go straight to Active. Active listings use the normal data entry form instead. [Form header](https://irp.cdn-website.com/3d0f9886/files/uploaded/Multiple+Options+for+Sellers+June+2026.pdf); [Coming Soon page comparison table](https://www.stellarmls.com/coming-soon-listings)

**Who signs.** All owners initial and sign. The listing broker or office manager and the agent sign too, with MLS ID numbers. The form says "no changes or strikethroughs will be accepted." [Form](https://irp.cdn-website.com/3d0f9886/files/uploaded/Multiple+Options+for+Sellers+June+2026.pdf)

**Pick one option only.** The four boxes:
1. **Coming Soon.** Entered in Stellar and available to all Stellar agents. The seller may allow public marketing while display on public-facing websites is delayed. Up to 14 calendar days from the on-market date, provided entry happens within 5 business days of the agreement start or within 1 business day of marketing. "All marketing must clearly identify the property as 'Coming Soon.'" Separate Yes/No line for showings.
2. **Delayed Distribution.** Entered and shared with all Stellar agents. Public marketing and public website display are delayed "for 5 calendar days from the Listing Agreement Beginning Date." Note that the clock runs from the agreement date on the form, not from MLS entry. The broker may otherwise market as the seller directs.
3. **Office Exclusive.** Entered in Stellar for the whole listing period but viewable only by the listing brokerage. No public marketing. Any public marketing triggers release to all Stellar agents.
4. **Office Exclusive with Temporary Exclusion.** Visible only to the broker and the listing agent until a Temporary Exclusion Date the seller writes in. Kept off IDX and public sites. After that date it automatically reverts to Office Exclusive. Public marketing during the exclusion means release to all agents within 1 business day.

**What every seller acknowledges.** These lines are marked "(Required)."
- Fair housing. The seller affirms the home is not being kept off the MLS to avoid showing, listing or selling to anyone in a protected class.
- A professional relationship exists between the seller and the listing broker.
- Unrestricted MLS exposure is "widely regarded as the single most effective means" to maximize reach, speed and price. By limiting or delaying it, the seller assumes "full responsibility for any extended marketing time, reduced buyer interest, or the adverse impact on final sales terms."

**Extra acknowledgments, Coming Soon and Delayed Distribution only.** The listing goes into Stellar and is open to all Stellar agents. Public display may be limited or delayed. Coming Soon shows as "Coming Soon" for 14 days unless changed. Neither option waives MLS submission.

**Extra acknowledgments, Office Exclusive and Temporary Exclusion only.**
- The seller is "knowingly and voluntarily relinquishing the substantial advantages of full MLS distribution," listed as exposure to 80,000+ licensees, the widest online visibility, and competition that drives "quicker showings, stronger offers, and potentially higher sale prices."
- Any public marketing triggers full MLS entry within 1 business day.
- The form's definition of public marketing. Window flyers, yard signs, public websites, "all social media platforms public or private," brokerage websites including IDX and VOW, email or text blasts, automated voice calls, "multi-brokerage listing sharing networks," and public apps.

**Who keeps it.** The listing broker keeps the fully signed original on file. Agents do not upload it routinely. "The listing Participant must retain a fully signed copy on file." [Art. 05.11(2)](https://rules.stellarmls.com/article/article-0511-MLS-Listing-Options-for-Property-Owners)

**Deadlines.**
- Listing entry, including Office Exclusive and Temporary Exclusion entries, within 5 business days of the owner's signature or agreement start. [Form](https://irp.cdn-website.com/3d0f9886/files/uploaded/Multiple+Options+for+Sellers+June+2026.pdf); [Art. 05.01(A)](https://rules.stellarmls.com/article/article-0501-submission-of-listings)
- Copy to Stellar within 2 business days of a request. Level II fine if late. [Art. 05.11](https://rules.stellarmls.com/article/article-0511-MLS-Listing-Options-for-Property-Owners)
- Office Exclusive and Temporary Exclusion listings need a price and one front exterior photo. Days on market count while excluded and reset to zero on release. [Delayed Distribution page, OE/W3, W7, W9](https://www.stellarmls.com/DelayedDistribution), Oct 2025

**The Florida Realtors listing agreement lines up with this.** Florida Realtors updated the Exclusive Brokerage Listing Agreement to EBLA-13 and related listing forms (LSLA-5, VLLA-6, ERS-10cp and others) "to incorporate the NAR MLS Policy on Multiple Listing Options for Sellers," effective Jan 5, 2026. [Florida Realtors, Jan 2, 2026](https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/01/florida-realtors-rolls-out-new-updated-forms). EBLA-13 Rev 1/26 text, from [Florida Realtors' redline](https://www.floridarealtors.org/sites/default/files/2025-12/Exclusive%20Brokerage%20Listing%20Agreement_EBLA-13_redlined_1.pdf):
- Paragraph 5. The broker must enter the property within 1 business day of public marketing "or as necessary to comply with local MLS rule(s)." Seller and broker "agree to adhere to each local MLS's policies and further agree to execute any applicable forms as necessary." That is the hook for the Stellar form.
- Paragraph 6(a)(ii). "Public marketing also includes marketing the Property to real estate agents outside Broker's office."
- Paragraph 6(b), the Seller Opt-Out. If the seller checks it, there is no For Sale sign and the broker may market "only to agents within Broker's office."
- A teaching point. The Florida Realtors contract counts marketing to agents outside your office as public marketing. Stellar allows one-to-one broker-to-broker conversations about an Office Exclusive (see question 5). Train agents to stay inside the stricter line, and to send gray-area calls to the broker.

## 4. Miami and BeachesMLS rules, for agents who list outside Stellar

### The two are now one association

MIAMI REALTORS® and RWorld, the Broward, Palm Beaches and St. Lucie association that runs BeachesMLS, closed their merger on May 11, 2026. They describe the result as "a single association and MLS" of about 93,000 members. The combined MLS name was not yet set. Members were promised IDX feeds that "will soon include all the listings of both MLSs" and a choice of Flexmls or Matrix. Teresa King Kinney and Dionna Hall are co-CEOs through 2026, with Hall sole CEO from 2027. Primary. [Miami Realtors release, May 12, 2026](https://www.miamirealtors.com/2026/05/12/miami-realtors-and-rworld-complete-historic-merger-creating-the-worlds-largest-local-realtor-association/); [Florida Realtors, Apr 2026](https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/04/south-florida-association-merger-sets-record)

I found no merged rulebook. As of today the two legacy rule sets, MIAMI MLS (SEFMLS) and BeachesMLS, still appear separately. Treat everything below as legacy-MLS rules that may change when the books merge.

### MIAMI MLS (SEFMLS). Most permissive in Florida, and harder to verify.

Miami's rule pages sit behind a member login, so I could see some items only in search snippets.
- **Office exclusives.** Per Miami Realtors' Sept 17, 2025 notice "Your MLS, Your Choice," there were no changes to Office Exclusive rules since the Aug 17, 2024 SEFMLS update. Entry into SEFMLS "is not required" and "public marketing is permitted." A signed Office Exclusive seller authorization must reach the MLS within 2 business days of the listing agreement's effective date under Rule 15.1.1. [Miami Realtors, Sept 17, 2025](https://www.miamirealtors.com/2025/09/17/your-mls-your-choice-minimal-updates-to-mls-rules-forms-go-live-on-10-14/) [UNCONFIRMED; member-gated, snippet only]. Two independent sources line up with it. Compass names Miami as permissive (above). A Miami brokerage blog describes two Miami forms, an Opt-Out form and a "Private Exclusive Marketing Authorization" for marketing outside the MLS. [Opes Group, Nov 21, 2025](https://opesre.com/miami-private-exclusives-rules-2025/)
- **Coming Soon.** Miami kept its existing Coming Soon status, up to 21 days, and added no new statuses for NAR's seller-options policy. Changes went live Oct 14, 2025. [Miami Realtors, Sept 17, 2025](https://www.miamirealtors.com/2025/09/17/your-mls-your-choice-minimal-updates-to-mls-rules-forms-go-live-on-10-14/) [snippet]. An older Miami workbook says Coming Soon listings cannot be shown or publicly marketed and may be used once per calendar year per property. Background, 2022. [MLS and You workbook](https://www.miamirealtors.com/wp-content/uploads/bsk-pdf-manager/2022/02/MLS-and-You-Workbook-2022-REVISED.pdf) [snippet]
- **Fines.** The Miami help center lists Clear Cooperation fines of $2,500 first, $5,000 second, and $7,500 plus MLS Review Panel third. [Miami Realtors help center](https://miamirealtors.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/37486599533595-MLS-Fines) [UNCONFIRMED; page blocked, snippet only, about 2025]. Coming Soon violations reportedly run $1,000, waived if fixed within 2 days of notice, then $2,500 and $5,000. [UNCONFIRMED; garbled snippet]
- **New Miami explainer.** On Jan 30, 2026 Miami Realtors released a new reference form, "Listings: Where do they go? What are the rules?" It covers status, sharing, and what is "ok to advertise." [Miami Realtors MLS Briefing Week 62, Jan 30, 2026](https://www.miamirealtors.com/2026/01/30/mls-briefing-week-62-2/). I could not read the form itself.

### BeachesMLS, Broward, Palm Beach and St. Lucie

- **Coming Soon.** Go-active date of 21 days or less from list date, then automatic Active with no going back. No private or public showings, open houses or broker opens. Photos not required. Days on market do not count. Goes to broker IDX feeds but not syndication unless the broker opts in. [BeachesMLS Rules, updated Apr 24, 2025](https://fiddle-clementine-dk98.squarespace.com/s/BeachesMLS-Rules-and-Regulations-2025.pdf)
- **Delayed Marketing.** This is a Yes/No field inside Coming Soon, not a separate status. With Delayed Marketing = Yes the listing stays out of IDX, hides history, and allows showings and open houses, still capped at 21 days. A signed "Seller's Delayed Marketing Disclosure" is required before entry. Brokers may write their own. Missing seller authorization means a "$1,000 fine." [RWorld Delayed Marketing page](https://rworld.com/dmel), undated, current site
- **Office exclusive.** Filed with the MLS but not shared with participants, with a seller certification. Copy due when the MLS asks. [BeachesMLS Rules Sec. 3.3, Apr 24, 2025](https://fiddle-clementine-dk98.squarespace.com/s/BeachesMLS-Rules-and-Regulations-2025.pdf)
- **Clear Cooperation.** 1 business day after public marketing, same list of public marketing channels as Stellar, including "multi-brokerage listing sharing networks." [Sec. 3.19](https://fiddle-clementine-dk98.squarespace.com/s/BeachesMLS-Rules-and-Regulations-2025.pdf). Fine is $1,000 per Compliance Guidelines 4.2k. Same-broker-of-record sister brokerages may share. [RWorld Clear Cooperation page](https://rworld.com/blog/clear-cooperation-policy), background, written for the 2020 rollout

**Cross-MLS access.** Stellar agents can already search Miami and BeachesMLS listings through reciprocal access deals. Miami with Stellar was announced Sept 22, 2025. BeachesMLS with Stellar on Oct 15, 2025, about 70,600 BeachesMLS listings visible to Stellar users. [Stellar and Miami, HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/stellar-mls-miami-realtors-reciprocal-access-agreement/); [Stellar and BeachesMLS, Stellar news, Oct 15, 2025](https://www.stellarmls.com/beachesmls-and-stellar-mls-announce-major-data-share-expansion-connecting-agents-and-listings-across-florida). These are access agreements. A Stellar agent taking a South Florida listing still has to follow the rules of the MLS where the listing is filed. That last point is my inference.

## 5. What a Stellar agent can do before the listing is in the MLS

Sources for this section are [Art. 05.01](https://rules.stellarmls.com/article/article-0501-submission-of-listings), [Art. 05.11](https://rules.stellarmls.com/article/article-0511-MLS-Listing-Options-for-Property-Owners), the [Coming Soon page comparison table](https://www.stellarmls.com/coming-soon-listings), the [Clear Cooperation and Delayed Distribution FAQs](https://www.stellarmls.com/DelayedDistribution) from Oct 2025, and the [Definitions Guide](https://irp.cdn-website.com/3d0f9886/files/uploaded/CCP+Definitions+Guide+7.16.25.pdf) from July 2025.

### Allowed
- **Take the listing and wait up to 5 business days to enter it,** as long as nothing is marketed in that window. If the home is not ready, file it as Office Exclusive with Temporary Exclusion or Coming Soon. [FAQ CCP12, OE/W11](https://www.stellarmls.com/DelayedDistribution)
- **Talk about an Office Exclusive with agents inside your own brokerage, and show it to your own office's buyers.** Showing an Office Exclusive only to internal clients is not public marketing. [FAQ CCP18](https://www.stellarmls.com/DelayedDistribution)
- **Have one-to-one broker-to-broker conversations about an Office Exclusive.** Stellar cites NAR that "broker-to-broker communication" is permitted. Its comparison table marks one-to-one broker communication Yes for Office Exclusive and **No** for Temporary Exclusion. [FAQ OE/W6](https://www.stellarmls.com/DelayedDistribution); [Coming Soon page table](https://www.stellarmls.com/coming-soon-listings). Keep it to one broker at a time. The Florida Realtors listing agreement treats marketing to outside agents as public marketing.
- **Market publicly from day one with Coming Soon.** Signs, social posts and email, all labeled "Coming Soon," with entry within 1 business day. All Stellar agents can see it and can show it if the seller allows. [Coming Soon page](https://www.stellarmls.com/coming-soon-listings)
- **Market however the seller directs during Delayed Distribution,** including your own website, social media and open houses. The listing is already Active in the MLS. The 5-day delay applies only to IDX and portal feeds. [FAQ DD5 to DD8, DD11](https://www.stellarmls.com/DelayedDistribution)
- **Who inside the firm can see it.** Stellar's wording varies. The rule says an Office Exclusive is available only to "the listing broker's office," the seller form says "Broker and Agents of the listing brokerage," and an Oct 2025 FAQ says "only agents in the same office." For a one-office boutique the three readings match. A Temporary Exclusion is limited to the listing broker and the listing agent. [Art. 05.11(B) and (C)](https://rules.stellarmls.com/article/article-0511-MLS-Listing-Options-for-Property-Owners); [Form](https://irp.cdn-website.com/3d0f9886/files/uploaded/Multiple+Options+for+Sellers+June+2026.pdf); [FAQ OE/W4](https://www.stellarmls.com/DelayedDistribution)

### Not allowed
- Any public marketing of an Office Exclusive or Temporary Exclusion. That includes yard signs, window flyers, brokerage or IDX websites, email or text blasts, and **any social media, "public or private."** The rule gives you 1 business day to release the listing. [Form](https://irp.cdn-website.com/3d0f9886/files/uploaded/Multiple+Options+for+Sellers+June+2026.pdf); [FAQ CCP19](https://www.stellarmls.com/DelayedDistribution)
- **Private listing networks that reach outside your brokerage.** "Multi-brokerage listing sharing networks" count as public marketing. Sharing with a select group that includes agents from other brokerages triggers submission. [Art. 05.01](https://rules.stellarmls.com/article/article-0501-submission-of-listings); [FAQ CCP9](https://www.stellarmls.com/DelayedDistribution)
- Putting an Office Exclusive on your own brokerage website. [FAQ OE/W5](https://www.stellarmls.com/DelayedDistribution)
- Showing a Temporary Exclusion property. Stellar counts that as public marketing. [FAQ CCP18](https://www.stellarmls.com/DelayedDistribution)
- **Marketing with only a commission agreement signed.** "A commission agreement does not provide permission to market the property." [FAQ CCP20](https://www.stellarmls.com/clearcooperation)
- **Following a seller's or bank's request to break the rule.** If a bank requires a sign, the agent is still in violation. Send the bank the rules. [FAQ CCP17](https://www.stellarmls.com/DelayedDistribution)
- **Marketing a home as Coming Soon before it is in Stellar as Coming Soon.** You have 1 business day. [Coming Soon page FAQ 9.4](https://www.stellarmls.com/coming-soon-listings)

### Edge cases
- **No signed listing agreement yet.** Stellar says Clear Cooperation applies once all parties sign the listing agreement. Without one, it says to ask your broker or the Florida Realtors Legal Hotline. [FAQ CCP11](https://www.stellarmls.com/DelayedDistribution). Do not read this as permission to pre-market before signing. The new Florida Realtors listing agreement defines public marketing broadly.
- **Agent's own home.** Entry is optional when the agent personally owns it, but not when an LLC or other entity owns it. [Art. 05.01(B)](https://rules.stellarmls.com/article/article-0501-submission-of-listings)
- **The Delayed Distribution form and FAQ seem to disagree.** The form says the seller tells the broker "to delay any public marketing." The FAQ says the listing office has "no restrictions." Stellar's July 2025 guide reconciles the two. The delay applies to "public marketing via cooperating MLS Broker Participants and their agents (through IDX and syndication)." [Definitions Guide](https://irp.cdn-website.com/3d0f9886/files/uploaded/CCP+Definitions+Guide+7.16.25.pdf). For training, say the listing office can market. Other brokers' websites and the portals wait 5 days.
- **Stale Stellar pages.** The Clear Cooperation and Delayed Distribution pages, last updated Oct 2025, still say there is no Coming Soon status. Point agents to the rulebook at rules.stellarmls.com and the Coming Soon page.

### Slide-ready summary of Stellar's four options

| | Coming Soon | Delayed Distribution | Office Exclusive | Temporary Exclusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| In Stellar? | Yes, all agents see it | Yes, Active, all agents | Yes, your brokerage only | Yes, listing broker and agent only |
| Public marketing | Yes, labeled "Coming Soon" | Yes, by listing office | No | No |
| Showings | Seller's choice | Yes | Internal clients only | No, counts as public marketing |
| On IDX and portals | Per seller's distribution choice | After 5 calendar days | No | No |
| Max time | 14 days, then auto-Active | 5 days, no extension | Whole listing term | Until seller's chosen date, then becomes Office Exclusive |
| Days on market | Do not accrue | Accrue | Accrue, reset on release | Accrue, reset on release |
| Seller form | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Sources: [Coming Soon page table](https://www.stellarmls.com/coming-soon-listings); [Art. 05.11](https://rules.stellarmls.com/article/article-0511-MLS-Listing-Options-for-Property-Owners); [FAQ OE/W7](https://www.stellarmls.com/DelayedDistribution).
