# Compass and the Compass-owned Anywhere brands in Florida (focus: Orlando, Kissimmee/Osceola, Tampa Bay)

Research date: October 27, 2026. Scope: Florida only. Feeds the Florida slides of the reThought Real Estate training deck.

How to read this file:
- Every fact has a source link and date. "Fetched Oct 27, 2026" means I read the live page on that date.
- **[UNCONFIRMED]** = I saw it only in a search snippet, a secondary or self-interested source, or the page could not be opened (paywall/403). Do not put these on a slide without a caveat.
- **Company claim** = the number comes from the company itself and nobody else has checked it.
- Already-known facts from the brief (CB Realty Orlando/Tampa offices, ONE Sotheby's takeover Sept 9, 2026, the $475 fee suit, Florida Realtors Hispanic data) are not repeated except where I found something new or contradictory.
- The sibling folder `research_notes/Compass and Open Real Estate/` already covers national context (deal terms, Stellar Clear Cooperation rules, Miami-Dade rankings, Open Real Estate basics). This file adds Central Florida detail.

**The most important rule for the deck:** most Compass-family yard signs in Central Florida are **franchises**, and franchises are independently owned. Every Century 21 office page says "Each office is independently owned and operated." Premier Sotheby's pages say "Each franchise is independently owned and operated." The Coldwell Banker footer separates "company-owned offices," owned by a subsidiary of Anywhere Advisors LLC (now part of Compass), from "franchised offices, which are independently owned and operated." Only Compass-brand offices and Coldwell Banker Realty offices are owned by the parent company in Central Florida. As of Oct 27, 2026, I found no Sotheby's office in Central Florida that the parent owns.

## Q1. Which Compass-family brands and offices operate in Orlando / Central Florida / Tampa Bay, and who owns them?

### Takeaway
Two Compass-family brands are owned by the parent company in Central Florida: **Compass** itself (172 agents listed under Orlando and 310 under Tampa in Compass's own directory) and **Coldwell Banker Realty**, which has about 6 Orlando-area offices and about 9 Tampa Bay/Suncoast offices. Everything else is a franchise owned by local people: **Premier Sotheby's**, **Century 21**, **ERA**, **BHGRE**, **Corcoran**, and Coldwell Banker franchise offices. Century 21 is the densest franchise network in the Orlando/Kissimmee area, led by Century 21 Carioti with 5 offices including Celebration and Windermere. Premier Sotheby's (Naples) is a franchise and was **not** bought by the Compass-owned franchisor, unlike ONE Sotheby's on the East Coast.

## Q3. Florida-specific Compass news in 2026

### Takeaway
The Florida story is mostly about rules, not offices. In March 2026, Compass publicly named **Stellar MLS**, the MLS that reThought's Orlando agents use, as one of seven MLSs it says "double down" on rules against pre-marketing. As of Oct 23, 2026, Stellar has not sued Compass and Compass has not sued Stellar. Florida passed no private-listing law in 2026. Agent moves to and from Compass are concentrated in South Florida. I found no Central Florida office openings, closures or team moves in 2026.

### Cited findings
**Compass vs. Stellar MLS**
- Mar 19, 2026: Compass International Holdings, Rocket and Redfin sent an open letter naming **ARMLS, CRMLS, FMLS, Georgia MLS, NWMLS, OneKey MLS and Stellar MLS** as MLSs that "double down" on pre-marketing restrictions. The letter said they "represent a clear minority in an industry that is rapidly moving toward seller choice." No Stellar response was reported. Sources: [RISMedia, Mar 19, 2026](https://www.rismedia.com/2026/03/19/compass-pressures-mlss-who-double-down-on-premarket-restrictions-with-fiery-open-letter/); [Real Estate News, Mar 19–20, 2026](https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/03/19/compass-rocket-to-mls-leaders-we-can-no-longer-stand-idly-by)
- Litigation status: CRMLS sued Compass on Oct 5. Compass sued CRMLS on Oct 16. Six more MLSs sued Compass on Oct 22: ARMLS, BridgeMLS, REcolorado, Hive, Canopy and Doorify. **No Florida MLS is a party.** Sources: [Real Estate News, Oct 20, 2026](https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/10/20/onekey-is-latest-mls-to-strike-back-at-compass); [The Real Deal, Oct 22, 2026](https://therealdeal.com/national/2026/10/22/compass-sued-by-six-more-multiple-listing-services/)
- Stellar's current options (July 6, 2026): Active, Coming Soon, **Delayed Distribution** (live to Stellar agents, held off IDX/portals for **5 calendar days**, "remains compliant with Clear Cooperation"), and **Office Exclusive** (no public syndication) — [Stellar MLS, Jul 6, 2026](https://www.stellarmls.com/more-ways-to-market-listings-with-stellar-mls)
- A Central Florida Realtor's guest post says that under Stellar rules, once a home is publicly marketed it must enter the MLS within one business day, and that "private listing networks" spanning more than one brokerage count as public marketing (opinion; the author discloses he works at NextHome, owned by AGNT, an Open Real Estate founder) — [Housing Notes, Oct 17/19, 2026](https://housingnotes.com/p/compass-cant-sell-enough-homes-so)

## Q4. Florida data on private/off-MLS listings and on Hispanic sellers being steered

### Takeaway
**I found no 2025–2026 Florida-, Orlando- or Tampa-specific measure of private or off-MLS listings, and no Florida-specific data on Hispanic sellers being steered.** The best evidence is national. It shows Hispanic neighborhoods take the largest price hit from private listings, which matters for a bilingual brokerage in a state with 6.7M Hispanic residents. Present it as national data, never as Florida data.

### Cited findings
- **ARELLO white paper (Sept 18, 2026; Zillow data; >10M sales, Jan 2024–Jun 2026; national only):** private listings sold at a median **0.88% below** standard MLS listings in majority-white neighborhoods, **2.21% below** in majority non-white neighborhoods, and **3.33% below in majority-Hispanic neighborhoods**. Coming-soon listings sold about 1.16–1.55% *above* standard listings. ARELLO says the gaps "do not establish causation or unlawful discrimination." Sources: [Real Estate News, Sept 23, 2026](https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/09/23/report-coming-soons-sell-for-more-than-private-standard-listings); [HousingWire, Sept 23, 2026](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/arello-private-listings-study/)
- **Zillow/Harris Poll survey (Jan 2025, 2,087 sellers, national):** **74% of Hispanic sellers** said their agent recommended a private listing network, vs. 24% of white sellers. Zillow also found off-MLS sales in majority-Hispanic areas sold about 4% lower. Zillow has a commercial stake in this fight. Source: [Latin Times, Oct 21, 2026](https://www.latintimes.com/private-listing-gap-how-off-mls-networks-are-quietly-costing-minority-sellers-thousands-600400)
- **Compass's own data (company claim, national, no Florida breakdown):** pre-marketed listings sold for 4.6% more (70,809 sales, Apr 2025–Mar 2026) — [Compass, Jul 24, 2026](https://www.compass.com/newsroom/research/sellers-make-four-point-six-percent-more-with-private-exclusives-or-coming-soons). 6.6% of Compass "3-Phased Marketing" transactions sold off-MLS in 2025 — [Compass, Oct 12, 2026](https://www.compass.com/newsroom/research/demystifying-the-off-mls-universe/)

The count includes FSBO, wholesale and investor deals, not only brokerage pocket listings [dated Aug 26, 2024; secondary] — [ProBuilder, Aug 26, 2024](https://www.probuilder.com/business-management/market-data-trends/article/55229334/how-common-are-market-home-sales)
