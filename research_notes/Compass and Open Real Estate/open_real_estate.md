# Open Real Estate: what it is and how it relates to Compass and the listing-access fight

Research date: October 26, 2026, which is the same day Open Real Estate was announced. Coverage is still first-day reporting, so expect details to change.

Confidence in the identification: **high.** One entity fits the user's description: Open Real Estate (openrealestate.com). Four national brokerage/franchise groups announced it on October 26, 2026. It is a shared listing-data initiative aimed at the rise of private listings, the practice Compass has led.

## 1. What is Open Real Estate, when was it announced, and who is behind it?

### Takeaway
Open Real Estate is a proposed national listing-data-sharing platform run as an industry consortium. It is not a brokerage, a franchise, a portal, or an MLS. AGNT Inc. (eXp Realty/NextHome), HomeServices of America, Keller Williams, and Real REMAX Group announced it on October 26, 2026. As of today it is a plan: no software exists yet, a tech vendor is due to be chosen in Q1 2027, and governance is still being written.

Headline: "Leading Real Estate Firms Partner to Form Open Real Estate, a National Property Data-Sharing Initiative." The release calls it a "national initiative" to create "a shared infrastructure for property listing data." — [Business Wire release via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/real-estate/articles/leading-real-estate-firms-partner-134500268.html); also syndicated at [Joplin Globe](https://www.joplinglobe.com/region/national_business/leading-real-estate-firms-partner-to-form-open-real-estate-a-national-property-data-sharing/article_661a7ee4-008d-5cc9-8172-db1c4bfcbf3b.html)

### Inferences
- The accurate category is "brokerage-led listing-data consortium/infrastructure." The closest analogy is a voluntary, national, broker-owned listing exchange that sits beside the MLS. Calling it a "new company" is loosely correct, since it appears to be a new entity, but agents should not be told it is a brokerage they can join or a website buyers can search.
- None of the founders is Zillow, Redfin/Rocket, NAR, an MLS, or a former Anywhere brand. The founders are the largest non-Compass franchisors and brokerages: KW, eXp, RE/MAX/Real, and HomeServices (the Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices family).

## 2. How is it related to Compass, the Anywhere acquisition, and the private-listing / Clear Cooperation fight?

### Takeaway
Open Real Estate is a direct counter-move to the private-listing model Compass has championed, though its own materials never name Compass. It has no link to the Anywhere deal itself: it is not a spin-off and not an exodus of Anywhere franchisees. Compass was invited to be a founding member and has not joined. A Compass spokesperson publicly praised it anyway, while Compass keeps suing MLSs over rules similar to Open Real Estate's own.

### Cited Findings
- **Stated reason:** the founders cite the rise in private exclusives. The release: "Today, more homes are being marketed privately, creating greater inconsistency..." — [Real Estate News](https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/10/26/4-major-brokerage-firms-unveil-open-real-estate-listings-platform); [Chicago Agent](https://chicagoagentmagazine.com/2026/10/26/open-real-estate/)
- **Compass not named:** the release does not mention Compass, Zillow, NAR, or Clear Cooperation. — [Business Wire via Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/real-estate/articles/leading-real-estate-firms-partner-134500268.html). Chicago Agent's writer calls Compass's pursuit of private listings, including its Private Listing Network, a key driver. — [Chicago Agent](https://chicagoagentmagazine.com/2026/10/26/open-real-estate/)
- **Compass invited:** Poleg said Compass International Holdings was invited to be a founding member. The leaders did not share Compass's answer. — [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/open-real-estate-initiative/)
- **Compass's statement (Real Estate News, attributed to an unnamed Compass International Holdings spokesperson):** "Compass International Holdings is excited that there will be a national real estate listing service that returns power to the creator of the listing — the homeowner and their trusted agent. This is good news for all agents and their clients ... we look forward to helping build the next chapter of listing services." — [Real Estate News](https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/10/26/4-major-brokerage-firms-unveil-open-real-estate-listings-platform)
  - **Attribution conflict:** MindCast attributes the statement to CEO Robert Reffkin, signing as Compass International Holdings. — [MindCast AI](https://www.mindcast-ai.com/p/compass-open-real-estate-endorsement-without-entry). HousingWire says Compass did not respond to its request for comment. — [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/open-real-estate-initiative/)
- **The Anywhere link is indirect.** Compass completed its acquisition of Anywhere Real Estate on January 9, 2026 (BAM reports $1.6B). The combined holding company, Compass International Holdings, owns brands including Coldwell Banker and Sotheby's International Realty. — [BAM](https://nowbam.com/robert-reffkins-letter-sets-the-tone-after-compass-closes-1-6b-anywhere-merger/); [MindCast AI](https://www.mindcast-ai.com/p/compass-open-real-estate-endorsement-without-entry). No Anywhere/Compass franchise brand appears among Open Real Estate's founders or initial participants. — [openrealestate.com](https://openrealestate.com)
- **Litigation backdrop:**
  - Compass sued CRMLS on October 16 over listing rules. — [MindCast AI citing Inman](https://www.mindcast-ai.com/p/compass-open-real-estate-endorsement-without-entry)
  - Six MLSs representing more than 120,000 professionals filed a joint action against Compass on October 21 to uphold cooperation rules. — [BAM](https://nowbam.com/4-major-brokerages-join-forces-to-launch-open-real-estate-amid-private-listing-battles/); [MindCast AI](https://www.mindcast-ai.com/p/compass-open-real-estate-endorsement-without-entry)
  - OneKey MLS (New York) rejected Compass's demand to change its listing-sharing rules on October 20, 2026. — [Yahoo Finance / OneKey release](https://finance.yahoo.com/real-estate/articles/onekey-mls-rejects-compass-demand-100000093.html); [St. Louis Real Estate News](https://stlouisrealestatenews.com/real-estate-market/another-major-mls-stands-up-to-compass-and-defends-an-open-real-estate-marketplace/)
  - Compass is in court with MLSs and Zillow over rules limiting how brokerages can market private listings before sharing them broadly. — [Real Estate News](https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/10/26/4-major-brokerage-firms-unveil-open-real-estate-listings-platform)
- **The rule tension:** Open Real Estate requires participants to enter a listing into its database "within one business day of the listing agreement being executed with a homeowner." — [FAQ](https://openrealestate.com/faq). MindCast argues this is stricter than the CRMLS rule Compass is suing over, which requires submission once public marketing starts. It also argues Compass's "Coming Soon" phase (public on Compass.com with days on market hidden) has no equivalent inside Open Real Estate. — [MindCast AI](https://www.mindcast-ai.com/p/compass-open-real-estate-endorsement-without-entry). (This is opinion analysis, not neutral reporting.)
- **Partly-private option:** a listing set to "participant view" is visible only to agents at participating brokerages and "cannot be publicly marketed by anyone," including the listing agent. A "market" listing is public and can go out through MLS IDX. — [FAQ](https://openrealestate.com/faq)

### Inferences
- In plain terms: the largest non-Compass firms are building their own shared pool of listings so that buyers and agents outside Compass are not shut out of inventory. Their pitch is "share everything among members, quickly," set against Compass's "keep it in-house first."
- The "participant view" tier means the coalition does not ban pre-marketing. It moves the private phase from inside one brokerage to inside a large group of competing brokerages. That is a middle path between full MLS exposure and Compass-only exposure.
- Open Real Estate cannot pull Compass's (or Coldwell Banker's or Sotheby's) private listings into view unless those firms join. It widens access among participants; it does not force Compass's inventory open. MindCast speculates that a Compass-owned franchise brand might join while the Compass brokerage stays out. That is unconfirmed. — [MindCast AI](https://www.mindcast-ai.com/p/compass-open-real-estate-endorsement-without-entry)
- It is not part of the Clear Cooperation Policy, which is an NAR/MLS rule. It does run on a similar idea (share listings quickly and broadly), and its one-business-day rule is a private, voluntary version of that idea.

### Gaps
- Compass has not said whether it accepted or declined the founding-member invitation.
- Whether any MLS has agreed to accept listings from Open Real Estate is not public. Real Estate News and Chicago Agent both flag this. — [Real Estate News](https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/10/26/4-major-brokerage-firms-unveil-open-real-estate-listings-platform); [Chicago Agent](https://chicagoagentmagazine.com/2026/10/26/open-real-estate/)
- How participant-view listings interact with MLS rules that require submission within a set time is not explained.

## 3. Business model, how to join, cost, markets, and Florida/Wisconsin

### Takeaway
Brokerages and franchisees join free; right now that means signing a non-binding letter of support. Individual agents can only sign up for updates. Listings stay owned by the listing broker. Money from the pooled data is to be split equally per listing, so small firms are paid the same per listing as giants. The scope is national. No source names Florida or Wisconsin, but all four founders operate nationwide.

### Cited Findings
- **Cost:** "Open Real Estate invites every brokerage to join as a free participating member." — [Business Wire via Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/real-estate/articles/leading-real-estate-firms-partner-134500268.html). HousingWire's interviewees confirmed joining costs nothing. — [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/open-real-estate-initiative/)
- **Eligibility:** "All licensed and participating residential real estate brokerages or franchises, and their corresponding agents." The FAQ does not list MLSs as participants. — [FAQ](https://openrealestate.com/faq). The site's letter-of-support form is addressed to brokerages, franchisees, and MLSs. — [openrealestate.com](https://openrealestate.com)
- **How to join today:**
  - Brokerages, franchisees, and MLSs sign a letter of support at openrealestate.com (#join). It is "a non-binding statement of intent" to support the principles and help shape governance over the next six months. Signers agree to have their name and logo shown publicly as supporters.
  - Agents sign up (#agents) for development updates and informational sessions.
  - Source for both: [openrealestate.com](https://openrealestate.com)
- **Participant rules stated so far:**
  - Submit all public listings to Open Real Estate regardless of where they originated.
  - Listings that did not originate in Open Real Estate must reach it within one business day of going public.
  - Follow its required data fields.
  - Sources: [openrealestate.com](https://openrealestate.com); [FAQ](https://openrealestate.com/faq). HousingWire calls the one-business-day submission the "main rule." — [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/open-real-estate-initiative/)
- **Routing:** listings can be entered into Open Real Estate, which pushes them to MLSs and portals, or into a local MLS, which feeds portals and Open Real Estate. — [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/open-real-estate-initiative/). The founders will keep posting to local MLSs. — [Chicago Agent](https://chicagoagentmagazine.com/2026/10/26/open-real-estate/)
- **Syndication control:** "Open Real Estate has full discretion over where the database's listings are syndicated." — [FAQ](https://openrealestate.com/faq). Brokers choose at input between participant-only visibility and syndication. — [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/open-real-estate-initiative/)
- **Ownership and revenue:**
  - Listings are owned by the listing brokers. Pareja said the data will be monetized, but how is unclear.
  - Proceeds are shared equally among participating brokers, per listing: a 100-listing brokerage is paid the same per listing as one with 10,000. Pareja described the goal as to "democratize the monetization."
  - Source for both: [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/open-real-estate-initiative/)
  - The website says only that participants "share in value generated by the listings they contribute," with "details ... finalized as the initiative proceeds." — [openrealestate.com](https://openrealestate.com)
- **Antitrust disclaimer:** each company decides independently whether to take part. Participation involves no agreement on commissions, fees, pricing, services, customers, or markets. — [openrealestate.com](https://openrealestate.com)
- **What it promises each audience (from the open letter and site):**
  - **Sellers:** "Homeowners deserve clear, complete information about how their homes will be marketed," including expected exposure and the trade-offs of each approach. — [HousingWire open letter](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/open-real-estate-listing-access/)
  - **Buyers:** "Buyers deserve fair and comprehensive access to available housing information." — [Business Wire via Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/real-estate/articles/leading-real-estate-firms-partner-134500268.html)
  - **Agents and brokerages:** enter a listing once, with standardized fields, shared with all members. "All members (and users) should have full visibility to all homes available for sale." — [Business Wire via Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/real-estate/articles/leading-real-estate-firms-partner-134500268.html); [openrealestate.com](https://openrealestate.com)
  - **Small and independent firms:** the letter supports brands "of all sizes, in all locations across America." — [HousingWire open letter](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/open-real-estate-listing-access/). Pareja: "In other countries, behemoths have a monopoly and you can't enter as a small company." — [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/open-real-estate-initiative/)
- **Consumer site:** none announced. HousingWire describes a brokerage-facing interface and a planned "single input" for agents. Kelly suggested consumers will barely know it exists: "True success for them is that the system just works." — [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/open-real-estate-initiative/)
- **Markets:** described as national and residential. No specific markets are named. — [Business Wire via Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/real-estate/articles/leading-real-estate-firms-partner-134500268.html); [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/open-real-estate-initiative/)
- **Florida/Wisconsin:** a targeted search found no Florida- or Wisconsin-specific coverage, no statement from a Florida or Wisconsin MLS, and no Florida/Wisconsin brokerage among the named participants. — [search results incl. Stellar MLS pages](https://www.stellarmls.com/about/our-mission) (nothing relevant found)

### Inferences
- Keller Williams, eXp, RE/MAX/Real, and HomeServices all have national footprints, so the founders very likely have agents in Florida and Wisconsin. Open Real Estate's reach there will depend on those firms' local market share and on whether local MLSs (for example Stellar MLS or Miami in Florida, Metro MLS in Wisconsin) accept its feed. This is not confirmed by any source.
- What an agent can do **today**: sign up for updates. What a broker-owner can do **today**: sign the free, non-binding letter of support and help shape governance. Nobody can enter or search listings yet.
- "Equal per-listing revenue share" is the main pitch to independents and boutiques. They would get paid on data they currently hand to MLSs and portals for nothing. Amounts and timing are unknown.

### Gaps
- No fee schedule, revenue projections, data-licensing buyers, or payout mechanics have been published.
- Whether agents at non-participating brokerages can see "market" listings outside MLS/IDX is not specified.
- No Florida- or Wisconsin-specific participants, MLS agreements, or coverage were found.
- Total agent or listing counts for the founding group were not published in any source I read.

## 5. Are there other entities with similar names, and which one matches?

### Takeaway
The October 26, 2026 consortium at openrealestate.com is the match. The other similar names are older, unrelated companies, or the phrase "open real estate marketplace" used by MLSs in their own anti-Compass statements.

### Cited Findings

It is the only "new" entity tied to Compass and private listings. — [openrealestate.com](https://openrealestate.com); [Real Estate News](https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/10/26/4-major-brokerage-firms-unveil-open-real-estate-listings-platform)

This could easily be confused with the consortium. — [Yahoo Finance / OneKey](https://finance.yahoo.com/real-estate/articles/onekey-mls-rejects-compass-demand-100000093.html); [St. Louis Real Estate News](https://stlouisrealestatenews.com/real-estate-market/another-major-mls-stands-up-to-compass-and-defends-an-open-real-estate-marketplace/)
