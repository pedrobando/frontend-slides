# The Standard — Session 1 (Florida): Design

**Date:** 2026-09-21
**File:** `presentations/the-standard-fl-session-1.html`
**Series:** The Standard (General / Office / Ethics weekly section)
**Audience:** reThought Real Estate Florida office agents
**Length:** ~45–60 min, ~23 slides
**Wisconsin variant:** separate deck, built after Florida is approved.

## Purpose

Teach Florida agents their legal duties under Ch. 475 F.S., framed through the
broker of record's own statutory responsibilities — never as "my rules." Every
office standard (dotloop, O365 email, offer protocols, voicemail) flows from a
duty the law places on the broker and the licensee. Positive framing only: no
competitor tools named (no TransactionDesk, no Gmail mentions).

## Decisions (from Pedro)

- One session per office; Florida first.
- Series name: **The Standard**.
- English content with Spanish key-takeaway echo lines; standard bilingual bookends.
- Anonymized real scenarios as case studies (yes, including the
  negotiating-without-the-seller incident).
- Act 1 must elaborate the FULL broker responsibility list: agent documents,
  agent communications, escrow, advertising, supervision, records.
- New act between agency law and scenarios: **Buyer Broker Agreement** — not
  required by Florida law, but required by NAR (post-Aug 2024 settlement).
- Framing: "Because the law makes me responsible for X, we do Y — together."
